# Ways of working

How <TEAM-NAME> works: agreements, processes, roles and principles. This folder is the place for knowledge about the way of working, separate from individual projects.

Examples of what belongs here:
- The process from idea to delivery, with phases and decision points
- Roles and responsibilities
- Recurring meetings and working agreements
- Principles and starting points
- Guides and templates the team uses itself

What does not belong here: knowledge about a single project (that lives in `wiki/projects/<slug>/`) and decisions about a project (those live in `wiki/decisions/`).

Naming convention: `<topic>.md`, lowercase with hyphens. For a larger topic, use a subfolder with its own `README.md`.

This folder is filled via the ingest flow. Pages here are curated, so changes go through the share skill and the curator.
